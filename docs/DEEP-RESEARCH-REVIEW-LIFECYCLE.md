## Five fixes and their boundaries

| Observed failure | Implementation | Guardrail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Historical absence warnings survived updated evidence | Full-context verification can explicitly resolve/supersede individual stable issue IDs; ledger retains original text, context, decision, exact reader hash and evidence-catalog hash | Requires exact current evidence and reader witnesses; missing/duplicate IDs, unknown original claims, security findings and protected prerequisites fail closed |
| Coverage calls saw opaque IDs | Issue packets now include original claim text, evidence references, stage provenance, and affected findings/questions | Missing propositions remain unresolved; contextual source IDs are not proof of entailment |
| Stitched quotes failed exact coverage and erased all progress | Multiple separate exact reader spans, per-issue validation and idempotent deterministic findings | No fuzzy matching, whitespace normalization, guessed ellipses or fabricated spans; bad peers still block completion but do not erase valid peers |
| Earlier claim warnings blocked corrected readers forever | Final exact-reader resolutions retire only explicitly resolved earlier findings from active admission | Historical findings stay in audit; a clean review boolean alone cannot clear a prior warning; repaired bytes require a fresh review |
| Correctly withheld conclusions blocked useful research | Explicit finding dispositions distinguish report defects from disclosed uncertainty or unavailable model conclusions | Numerical/security errors and contradicted claims cannot be scoped away; deterministic financial eligibility and production acceptance remain unchanged |

Semantic entailment remains the full-context verifier's responsibility. Matching
quotes proves provenance, not economic truth. Coverage-only reviewers cannot
resolve factual issues. Any unresolved active material finding still prevents a
completed report. A complete conditional reader is not an accepted investment
assessment, a price target or evidence of funding adequacy.

## Audit and repair behavior

`reader_verification.json` retains the original factual review, proposed issue
decisions, effective issue states, scoped findings and exact candidate identity.
The audit keeps historical findings even after a successful resolution. Retired
warnings still present verbatim in the reader require a new repaired candidate;
the original candidate is never rewritten. Report-defect repair instructions no
longer become permanent financial limitations merely because they occurred once.

Unresolved financial prerequisites remain protected. An authored inference can be
explicitly conditional without being a verified fact. Missing historical usage
still prevents full acceptance and is never changed by reader-review decisions.

Limitation origins distinguish reviewed operating-package/review metadata from
deterministic financial restrictions. Retirement of the former requires an exact
witness containing the current independently reviewed package hash. If identical
text also comes from any protected origin, it cannot be retired. Unreviewed
numerical notes are withheld from origin metadata as well as ordinary prompts.

## Compatibility

Provider contracts move to `research-wire-v3`; engine identity moves to
`research-v2-preview-4`. New strict replies must supply the new fields explicitly;
old offline domain records retain defaults for inspection. Existing checkpoints
and recovery authorizations are not silently reinterpreted as approvals for the
new behavior. Any future live continuation needs a freshly validated compatible
plan and new bounded authorization, not an in-place upgrade of saved artifacts.
